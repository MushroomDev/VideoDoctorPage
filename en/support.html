FAQ

Most permission and workflow questions are answered here.

Does repair modify my original file?

No. Sources are opened read-only. Repaired output is written to the folder you choose in settings.

I get “can’t access file” / permission errors

Under the macOS sandbox, you must grant access by picking files or an output directory. Re-add videos via Add Video, or re-select the output folder, then retry.

Diagnosis shows issues but repair fails

Outcomes depend on damage. Missing moov, extreme truncation, or almost no usable media may fail. The UI explains why when possible. If prompted, try a healthy reference clip from the same device/settings.

What are Free limits? How do I get Pro?

Free: about 3 successful repairs per day and ~3 minutes per file; diagnosis remains available. Buy Pro in-app (one-time) for unlimited repairs and Repair All without Free quotas. Use Restore Purchases if needed.

Which formats are supported?

Common MP4 / MOV and many everyday containers are prioritized. Experimental extensions may warn about lower success rates. Output aims for a more compatible H.264 / AAC MP4.

Thumbnails don’t show

Severely damaged files may not yield a first frame, so a placeholder appears. Decodable files usually show a cover frame.

System requirements

macOS 12 or later (see the App Store page for the definitive requirement). Leave enough free disk space for batch jobs.
