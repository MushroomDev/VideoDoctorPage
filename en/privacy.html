Privacy notice

Last updated: 2026-10-14. This page explains how VideoDoctor handles your media and purchase information.

1. Media processing

VideoDoctor probes, diagnoses, and repairs on your Mac. Repair does not require uploading videos to our servers. Source files are accessed read-only; output is written to the folder you choose.

2. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your video contents for the purpose of repair
  • No account login is required to use local repair features

3. Purchases

If you buy Pro, payment and receipt validation go through Apple’s App Store / StoreKit. We unlock entitlements on this Mac; we do not run a separate account system.

4. On-device storage

The app may store job queue state, diagnosis summaries, output-folder preferences, and entitlement status locally so you can resume later. That data stays on your device.

5. Support communications

If you contact us by email or GitHub Issues, the information you choose to share (for example macOS version and a problem description) is used only to investigate and reply.
